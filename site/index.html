HOW TO READ THIS DATA

Methodology & limitations

Every metric should be reproducible and clearly labeled.

01

Official inputs

Bus-stop locations and public-space notices come from Zaragoza City Council. Predicted arrivals use the Avanza operator stop feed, with municipal API fallback. Bizi uses the GBFS provider feed.

02

Coverage over certainty

Missing predictions, unknown bike conditions and API errors remain separate from measured values. We never interpret missing values as zero.

03

Bus observations

Cached predictions are sampled from up to 180 of the roughly 983 published stops per capture. All separately announced vehicles are retained. Grey stops lack a verified prediction; on-demand browser requests may be restricted. A wait is not a timetable delay.

04

Street incidents

Published start and planned end dates determine whether a notice is current, upcoming or past. They are not proof of actual incident resolution or repair time. Categories are translated, while original notices remain linked.

05

History and freshness

GitHub Actions targets one capture every 30 minutes. Seven days of station-level observations and up to 90 days of aggregate history are retained. Missing readings and long gaps interrupt episode-duration estimates. Fault disappearance is not proof of repair.

06

More datasets

Air quality, parking and urban infrastructure are candidates for future integration. They will only become live sections after their endpoints and update frequency are verified.
